2. What Macrolia does

Macrolia helps you calculate and track calories, nutrients, food intake, recipes and personal nutrition goals. You can enter your own products and values, create recipes, calculate the portions you actually ate, plan the next day and track your weight.

5. Calculations and accuracy

Macrolia calculates from the information you enter: nutritional values, amounts, recipes and ingredients, weight or number of portions, the amount actually eaten, personal data and goals.

When you enter the values from the label of a specific product, Macrolia uses exactly those values.

Pre-loaded products use values from official food composition tables (USDA FoodData Central, the Swiss Food Composition Database and the German Nutrient Database BLS 4.0 of the Max Rubner-Institut), and a few special products use the values from the label of a named product. Each product shows its source. Products usually bought with a label (for example milk, cheese, ham) have average values, and your brand may differ. Natural products vary by variety, ripeness and origin, so the real values may differ slightly.

Values marked with ≈ (for example restaurant food or amounts estimated by eye) are approximate.

Daily goals are calculated with generally accepted formulas and are an estimate for an average person with your data. Your actual energy use and the actual pace of weight change may differ.

The accuracy of the results depends on the accuracy of the information entered. We do not guarantee the accuracy of information entered by you or stated by a product's manufacturer.

6. AI photos

Recognising a label or a dish from a photo uses artificial intelligence (AI). The result is a suggestion that may contain errors, such as a misread number on the label or an inaccurately estimated amount on the plate. Please check and, if needed, correct the values before you save them. The estimate of a dish from a photo is always approximate (≈).

Photograph only food and labels, without people, documents or other personal information.

The subscription includes 100 AI photos per calendar month: 70 for labels and 30 for meals. Monthly photos are valid until the end of that month, and unused ones do not carry over to the next month; on the 1st you start again with 70 for labels and 30 for meals. A photo in which AI did not recognise a label or food is not counted.

If you need more, you can buy an extra photo pack through Google Play. Photos from extra packs are used after the monthly ones, work for labels and meals, and unused ones carry over to the next month with no end date. You can use them while you have an active subscription; if the subscription stops, they are kept until you renew it. All other features work without AI too, with manual entry.

By choosing this option you confirm that you have read the warning that Macrolia does not recommend this mode for your data, and that you use it at your own risk. We recommend this mode only after consulting a doctor.
