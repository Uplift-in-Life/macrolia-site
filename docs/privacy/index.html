Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21.11.2026. Applies to the Macrolia app and the macrolia.app website.

1. In short

  • Everything you enter in Macrolia is stored only on your phone. We don't see it.
  • The exception is AI photos: the photo is sent for recognition and is not stored.
  • No ads, no tracking tools or behaviour analytics, no cookies on the website. We don't sell data.

2. Data controller

Macrolia (Uplift in Life), Vienna, Austria; the company's full details will be added once it is registered, email: support@upliftinlife.com. We are not required to have a data protection officer; for all questions, write to this email.

3. What data, where, why and for how long

  • Sex, age, height, weight, activity, goal: only on the phone; for the daily limit and macros; legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); kept until you delete them.
  • Diary, products, recipes, plan for tomorrow, weight, progress photos: only on the phone; for the core features; legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); kept until you delete them.
  • Burned calories and steps from Health Connect: only on the phone; for the daily limit; legal basis: explicit consent and Android permission; kept until you delete the data or stop access.
  • AI photo of a label or dish: passes through our server (Cloudflare) to Anthropic; to recognise the values or the food; legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and consent at the first AI photo; we don't keep it, Anthropic deletes it within 30 days.
  • AI photo counter (random app number, month, count) and photos from extra packs: our server (Cloudflare); for the monthly limit (40 labels + 20 meals) and bought photos; legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); the monthly counter is deleted after 40 days, the number of bought photos is kept until you use them.
  • IP address and technical request data for AI photos: Cloudflare; for delivery and protection against attacks; legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); we don't record them.
  • Whether the subscription is active: Google Play and the phone; for access to the paid features; legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for as long as the subscription lasts.
  • Emails to support@upliftinlife.com: the Zoho email service (data centres in the EU); to answer your question; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR; up to 3 years after the last message.
  • Visits to macrolia.app (IP address, browser, time): GitHub Pages hosting; to show and protect the website; legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); we have no access to this data, GitHub keeps it under its privacy policy.

Macrolia doesn't ask for your name, email, address or date of birth and has no user account. Reminders in the app are created on the phone itself and don't go through us.

4. Health data and your consent

Weight, food intake, body photos, burned calories and steps are health-related data, i.e. a special category of data under the GDPR. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give at first start. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data in Settings or uninstalling the app. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

5. Health Connect

If you turn on “Automatic tracking”, Macrolia reads from Health Connect only actively burned calories and steps, and only after your permission in Android. We write nothing to Health Connect.

We use this data solely for your daily limit in the app. It stays on the phone, is not sent to our server, not sold, not shared with others, not used for advertising and not used to train AI. Its use complies with Google's Health Connect policies, including the Limited Use requirements. You can stop access at any time in the app's or the phone's settings.

6. AI photos

When you photograph a label or dish with AI, the photo is sent encrypted to our server at Cloudflare and from there to the AI service Claude by Anthropic PBC (USA), only so that the food or the label's values can be recognised. Along with the photo, the app's language and a random app number are sent, without your name, email or other data about you.

We don't store the photo. Anthropic processes it on our behalf as a processor, doesn't use it to train AI and deletes it within 30 days. Before the first AI photo the app explains this and you can decline. Please photograph only the food, without people.

The result is a suggestion that you check and save. AI does not make decisions about you.

7. Backup

If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android keeps an encrypted copy of the data in your Google account so it can restore it on a new phone. This copy is stored by Google in your Google account; we have no access to it. You turn it off in the phone's Settings → Google → Backup.

If you save a backup to a file yourself, you choose where to keep it. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. The same applies to PDF and Excel reports that you download or send to someone.

8. Payments

Subscriptions and extra AI photo packs are bought through Google Play. Payment data is processed by Google as an independent controller under its Privacy Policy. We don't receive your card details, only information on whether the subscription is active and which packs were bought, and summarised sales reports from Google.

9. Who else receives data

Processors acting on our behalf, under a data processing agreement:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): our server for AI photos and the counter.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA): recognition of AI photos.
  • Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands): the support@upliftinlife.com email.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): hosting of the macrolia.app website.

Independent controllers: Google (Google Play, payments, Android backup), under your agreements with Google.

Data transfers to the USA are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We don't sell data or share it for advertising. If we add a new provider, we will update this Policy first.

10. Security

The connection to our server and to the website is encrypted (HTTPS). Data on the phone is protected by Android's security, so we advise you to lock your phone.

11. Children

Macrolia is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

12. Deleting data

You delete everything in Settings → “Delete all data” or by uninstalling the app. The data is permanently deleted from the phone. The Google backup and files you saved yourself are deleted separately. The subscription is cancelled in Google Play.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability (the backup file and the Excel reports serve this purpose), objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdrawal of consent at any time. Write to us at support@upliftinlife.com.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at), and you can also contact the authority in the country where you live.

14. Changes

When the app or the law changes, we update this Policy. We inform you about significant changes in the app, and if a change requires new consent, we will ask for it beforehand.
